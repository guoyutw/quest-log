# Development lifecycle

1. **Plan** — bounded change and acceptance evidence.
2. **Implement** — smallest change inside the boundary.
3. **Real-world Evidence** — exercise the actual path; mocks do not prove external behavior.
4. **Retro** — record learning only after genuinely complete work.
5. **Carry-forward** — choose the next bounded action without silently expanding scope.

For 0.1, Retro is deferred until all three gates pass.
